# THALAIVAR

SUPERSTAR • THE LEGEND • THE CELEBRATION. A gold-and-black cinematic theme for Superstar Rajinikanth fans:
a "Good morning, Thalaiva" greeting, the cinematic shelf with a frosted dock, a gold halo focus, and twelve
original 16:9 wallpapers that change with Chennai's sunrise and sunset (morning: dawn and blue; evening: gold,
sunset, Padayappa and Coolie; night: Baasha, royal purple, midnight star and Jailer 2). The clock screensaver
comes up over the wallpaper after 10 minutes on Home.

All twelve pictures are original, drawn from gradients, light and type by `tools/thalaivar/make_art.py`; see
CREDITS.md. Pair it with the Thalaivar Hub add-on (`addons/thalaivar-hub/`) for the films, news and screensavers.

Personal poster wallpapers: `tools/thalaivar/poster_walls.py` builds a second, local copy of this theme in
`themes-local/thalaivar-posters/` with each film's official poster in 3D over its backdrop, from TMDB, for your
own TV only. That folder is git-ignored and must not be committed or shared.
